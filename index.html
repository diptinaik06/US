About me

I'm not the easiest person to love.

And I'm not trying to pretend I am.

I'm a little clumsy. Sometimes immature. Sometimes emotional. And definitely an overthinker.

I can take one small thing, think about it for hours, create ten different possibilities in my head, and then just need someone to tell me what is actually going on.

I feel things deeply. When I care, I care a lot. When I love, I don't know how to do it halfway.

I'm ambitious too. I'm studying, building my career, working, learning, trying new things, making plans, changing them, figuring myself out and growing along the way.

I have big dreams. But I also have days when I don't feel strong at all. Days when I need reassurance. Days when I become quiet. Days when my thoughts become louder than reality.

I can be stubborn. I can be sensitive. I can overreact sometimes. I can make mistakes. I'm still growing, and I know that.

Sometimes I need to hear something twice before my overthinking finally lets it go.

But beneath all of that, I'm loyal. I remember the little things. I care about the people I love.

I want to support the person I'm with. I want to celebrate his wins, stand beside him when life gets difficult, listen to him, understand him and make him feel loved too.

A few things that feel like me.

I love the blue of the ocean. Coffee feels like comfort. I love nature, quiet places, long conversations and little moments that don't need to be photographed. I have my own friendships, my own world and my own life. A healthy relationship should have trust, not unnecessary restrictions.

But I'll be honest — I do have trust issues. Trust doesn't come automatically to me. It is built slowly through honesty, consistency and actions that match words.

Once I genuinely trust someone, though, I don't want to keep looking over my shoulder. I want peace.

I don't expect perfection from him, because I'm not perfect either. What I want is effort, honesty, maturity, loyalty and consistency.

Someone who can look at all of me — the confident woman, the ambitious woman, the emotional woman, the messy woman, the vulnerable woman — and still choose to understand me.

And I want to do the same for him. I want to know his fears, his bad days, his dreams, his insecurities, and the things he doesn't tell everyone.

When I finally feel safe with someone, I will love him wholeheartedly.

Not perfectly. But genuinely. Not temporarily. But loyally.

Not because I need someone to complete me, but because I want someone with whom I can build something real.

A relationship where we both have our own lives,
but still choose each other every day.
